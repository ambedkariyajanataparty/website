AJP Manifesto

Our vision for Social Justice, Equality and Constitutional Democracy

Our Vision

Ambedkar Janata Party (AJP) believes in the principles of the Indian Constitution: Justice, Liberty, Equality and Fraternity. Our mission is to build an inclusive society where every citizen has dignity, opportunity and equal rights.

Our Key Commitments

Constitutional Values

Protect democratic institutions and uphold the Constitution of India.

Social Justice

Fight discrimination and ensure dignity, equality and representation for all communities.

Education For All

Provide quality education, opportunities and support for students.

Employment & Economy

Create employment opportunities and support workers, farmers and youth.

Reservation & Representation

Protect constitutional reservations and ensure fair representation in all sectors.

Women & Youth Empowerment

Promote equal opportunities, safety and leadership for women and youth.

Join the Movement for a Just and Equal India

Together we can build a society based on the ideals of Dr. B.R. Ambedkar and the Constitution of India.
