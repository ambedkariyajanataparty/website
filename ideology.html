Ideology of Ambedkariya Janata Party

A Constitutional Vision Based on Justice, Equality, Liberty and Fraternity

Our Ideological Foundation

Ambedkariya Janata Party is inspired by the ideas and principles of Dr. B.R. Ambedkar, who believed that political democracy must be supported by social and economic democracy.

AJP believes that the Constitution of India is the guiding framework for protecting rights, ensuring equality and building a just society.

Our ideology focuses on dignity, equal opportunities and empowerment of every citizen irrespective of caste, religion, gender or economic background.

Ambedkarism

Following the principles of liberty, equality, fraternity and social justice as envisioned by Dr. B.R. Ambedkar.

Constitutional Values

Protecting democracy, fundamental rights, rule of law and constitutional institutions.

Social Justice

Creating equal opportunities and removing social barriers through justice and empowerment.

Equality

Every citizen deserves equal dignity, equal rights and equal opportunities.

Liberty

Protecting freedom of thought, expression, belief and individual dignity.

Fraternity

Promoting unity, brotherhood and mutual respect among all sections of society.

Our Approach Towards Society

📘 Education as a tool for empowerment and social transformation.

⚖ Justice for communities facing inequality and discrimination.

🏛 Strong democratic institutions based on constitutional principles.

🤝 Building an inclusive society where every person lives with dignity and self-respect.
