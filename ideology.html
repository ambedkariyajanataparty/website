AJP Ideology

Inspired by Dr. B.R. Ambedkar and the values of the Indian Constitution

Our Guiding Principles

Ambedkar Janata Party believes in building a society based on Justice, Equality, Liberty and Fraternity. Our ideology is rooted in constitutional democracy and the vision of social transformation.

Social Justice

We believe every citizen deserves dignity, equality and equal opportunities. We work against all forms of discrimination.

Constitutional Democracy

We stand for protecting the Constitution, democratic institutions and fundamental rights of every citizen.

Education & Empowerment

Education is the foundation of progress. We support opportunities for students, youth and future generations.

Economic Justice

We support policies that create employment, strengthen workers, farmers and economically weaker sections.

Equal Representation

Every section of society must have a meaningful voice in governance and decision-making.

Secular & Inclusive India

We believe in unity, respect for all communities and equality before the law.

Educate • Agitate • Organize

Following the path shown by Dr. B.R. Ambedkar, AJP aims to create awareness, encourage participation and organize people for a better democratic future.
