AJP Programs in Detail

Transforming society through education, constitutional awareness, leadership and public participation.

1. Constitutional Awareness

Educating every citizen about constitutional rights and duties.

AJP will organise Constitution awareness campaigns, public lectures, workshops and village meetings to educate citizens about their rights, duties and democratic responsibilities.

2. Education & Student Development

Building an educated and skilled generation.

The party will conduct educational guidance programmes, career counselling, scholarship awareness, competitive exam coaching support and leadership development for students.

3. Women Empowerment

Creating equal opportunities for women.

AJP will encourage women's leadership, self-help groups, legal awareness, financial independence and equal participation in governance.

4. Farmers & Workers Welfare

Supporting the backbone of the nation.

The party will promote policies that improve agriculture, workers' welfare, fair wages, labour rights and rural development.

5. Youth Leadership

Preparing future leaders.

Youth development programmes will focus on leadership, entrepreneurship, innovation, democratic participation and nation building.

6. Social Justice & Human Rights

Justice, equality and dignity for every citizen.

AJP will continue campaigns against discrimination, promote constitutional safeguards, strengthen human rights awareness and work towards an equal society.
