Our National Mission

Ambedkariya Janata Party is committed to building an India where constitutional values guide governance, social justice creates equal opportunities, and every citizen lives with dignity, security and self-respect.

We believe that democracy becomes meaningful only when liberty, equality, fraternity and justice reach every household.
