About Ambedkariya Janata Party

AJP is committed to building a just, equal and constitutional India by upholding the ideals of Dr. B. R. Ambedkar.

Social Justice

Ensuring equality, dignity and justice for every citizen without discrimination.

Education

Promoting quality education and constitutional awareness among all sections of society.

Unity

Strengthening fraternity and national integration through democratic values.
