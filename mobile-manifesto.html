Our Vision

Building an inclusive India inspired by Dr. B. R. Ambedkar and the Constitution of India.

Ambedkariya Janata Party (AJP) is committed to building an India where every citizen enjoys Justice, Liberty, Equality and Fraternity. We believe constitutional values must guide governance, public policy and national development.

Our vision is to create a society free from discrimination, where every individual has equal opportunities, dignity, quality education, meaningful employment and access to social justice.
