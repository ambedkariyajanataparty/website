About Ambedkar Janata Party (AJP)

A movement inspired by the Constitution of India and the vision of Dr. B.R. Ambedkar

Who We Are

Ambedkar Janata Party (AJP) is a political movement committed to Justice, Equality, Liberty and Fraternity. The party believes that the strength of India lies in protecting constitutional values and ensuring dignity and equal opportunities for every citizen.

Inspired by the thoughts and struggles of Dr. B.R. Ambedkar, AJP works towards building a society based on social justice, democracy and equal representation.

Our Foundation

Constitutional Values

We stand for the protection of the Indian Constitution and democratic institutions.

Social Justice

We work towards removing discrimination and creating equal opportunities.

Equality & Dignity

Every individual deserves respect, dignity and equal rights.

Our Vision

To create an inclusive India where every person has equal rights, equal opportunities and a meaningful voice in democracy.

Our Mission

  • Protect constitutional rights and democratic values
  • Promote education and employment opportunities
  • Work for social justice and equal representation
  • Empower youth, women, farmers and workers
  • Build a society based on equality and fraternity

Educate • Agitate • Organize

The guiding principle of AJP is to create awareness, encourage democratic participation and organize people for a just society.
