About Ambedkariya Janata Party

A Constitutional Movement for Social Justice, Equality and Human Dignity

Who We Are

Ambedkariya Janata Party is a political movement inspired by the thoughts, struggles and vision of Bharat Ratna Dr. B.R. Ambedkar.

AJP believes that the Constitution of India is the foundation for democracy, social justice and equal opportunities for every citizen.

Our mission is to build an India where every person can live with dignity, freedom and self-respect.

Our Vision

To create an equal and democratic society where justice, liberty, equality and fraternity guide the future of India.

Our Mission

To protect constitutional values, empower people through education and create opportunities for social and economic development.

Our Foundation

Inspired by Dr. B.R. Ambedkar's principles of Educate, Agitate and Organize.

Our Core Objectives

📘 Promote quality education and knowledge-based empowerment.

⚖ Strengthen social justice and protect constitutional rights.

💼 Create equal opportunities in employment and economic development.

🤝 Build unity among all communities through fraternity and mutual respect.

Constitutional Values

AJP stands for democracy, fundamental rights, rule of law and constitutional governance.

Social Transformation

Working towards removing discrimination and creating a society based on dignity and equality.

People's Movement

Encouraging citizens, youth and communities to participate in democratic transformation.
