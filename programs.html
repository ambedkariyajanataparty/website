AJP Public Programs

People-Centred Initiatives for Education, Social Justice and Empowerment

Our Mission Through Action

Ambedkariya Janata Party believes that political change must be supported by awareness, education, organisation and people's participation.

Our programs focus on empowering students, youth, women, workers and communities through knowledge, opportunities and democratic participation.

AJP works towards creating an informed, equal and empowered society based on constitutional values.

Ambedkar Education Program

Creating awareness about Dr. B.R. Ambedkar's ideas, Constitution and the importance of education among students and citizens.

Youth Leadership Program

Training young people in leadership, social responsibility and democratic participation.

Women Empowerment Program

Promoting equal opportunities, dignity, safety and greater participation of women in society.

Constitution Awareness Campaign

Spreading knowledge about fundamental rights, duties and constitutional responsibilities.

Social Justice Movement

Creating awareness against discrimination and working towards equality and fraternity.

Community Development Program

Supporting education, healthcare, employment and development initiatives at the community level.

Future Program Areas

📘 Student awareness and educational initiatives.

💼 Skill development and employment support.

⚖ Constitutional rights awareness programs.

🤝 Community welfare and public participation.

🌱 Sustainable social and economic development.
