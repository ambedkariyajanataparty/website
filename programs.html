AJP Programs

Programs dedicated to social justice, awareness and people's empowerment

Our Initiatives

Ambedkar Janata Party works towards creating awareness, empowering communities and strengthening democratic participation through various public programs.

Ambedkar Education Program

Promoting awareness about the Constitution, social justice and the ideas of Dr. B.R. Ambedkar among students and citizens.

Social Justice Campaigns

Working to create equality, dignity and opportunities for all sections of society.

Youth Empowerment

Encouraging young people to participate in democracy and develop leadership skills.

Women Empowerment

Supporting equal participation, safety and leadership opportunities for women.

People's Outreach

Connecting with communities, listening to public concerns and working for solutions.

Constitution Awareness

Educating citizens about constitutional rights, duties and democratic values.

Serve People • Strengthen Democracy

Through these programs, AJP aims to build awareness, encourage participation and create a more equal society.
