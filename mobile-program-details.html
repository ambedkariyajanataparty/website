AJP Programs in Detail

Empowering citizens through education, constitutional awareness and social action.

1. Constitutional Awareness

Building informed citizens through constitutional education.

AJP will organise Constitution awareness campaigns, workshops, seminars and public meetings to educate citizens about their rights, duties and democratic responsibilities.

2. Education & Student Development

Creating opportunities for every student.

Educational guidance, career counselling, scholarship awareness, skill development and leadership programmes will help students achieve their full potential.

3. Women Empowerment

Equal opportunities and leadership.

AJP supports women's leadership, self-help groups, legal awareness, financial independence and equal participation in public life.

4. Farmers & Workers Welfare

Supporting those who build the nation.

We will promote better agricultural policies, labour welfare, fair wages, rural development and stronger social security.

5. Youth Leadership

Preparing tomorrow's leaders.

Youth programmes will encourage innovation, entrepreneurship, democratic participation and responsible leadership.

6. Social Justice & Human Rights

Justice and equality for everyone.

AJP will continue campaigns against discrimination, strengthen constitutional safeguards and promote human rights across the country.
